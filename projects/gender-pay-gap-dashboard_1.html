EU Gender Pay Gap Analysis (2002–2024)

Five statistically tested findings from 22 years of Eurostat data across 27 EU member states — trend, sector, age, country, and regression views.

~2100 projected parity

At the current pace, equality is roughly 74 years away

The gap closed ~5 points in the nine years to 2011 — then only 3.4 points in the years since 2015. Projecting the post-2015 rate forward, the EU average doesn't reach zero until around 2100.
