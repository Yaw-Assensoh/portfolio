For years I owned and operated a logistics business in Hamburg — optimising routes, forecasting demand, and segmenting customers using whatever data tools I had. Every decision had a real cost.

That pressure taught me something most analysts learn in theory: data only matters when it changes a decision.

What I learned running the business
Manual route planning cost real money — the kind of problem I now solve with optimisation models
Demand was seasonal and I under- or over-staffed constantly — see my forecasting case study
I could tell which customers were valuable but couldn't systematically act on it — this is exactly what segmentation addresses

US Housing Affordability

Method: K-Means segmentation + SARIMA forecasting

★ Featured Statistics · Labour Economics

EU Gender Pay Gap (2002–2024)

Question: Is the EU gender pay gap actually closing, and which sectors and age groups are driving what remains?

Method: Regression + t-tests + 22 years of Eurostat data

Finding: 5 statistically supported findings: trend, sector, age, country, and residual

Decision: Prioritise Finance sector monitoring and target age-related policy interventions

10.5% EU average · 24.1% in Finance
4.6% → 13.9% age-related increase
