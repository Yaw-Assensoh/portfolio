For years I owned and operated a logistics business in Hamburg — optimising routes, forecasting demand, and segmenting customers using whatever data tools I had. Every decision had a real cost.

That pressure taught me something most analysts learn in theory: data only matters when it changes a decision.

What I learned running the business
Manual route planning cost real money on bad days — the kind of problem I now solve with optimisation models instead of gut feel
Demand was seasonal and I under- or over-staffed for it constantly — see my forecasting case study for how I'd approach that today
I could tell which customers were valuable but couldn't systematically act on it — this is exactly what the segmentation project addresses

My Story

From logistics founder to data analyst

Transitioning into analytics wasn't a career change — it was adding Python, SQL, and Tableau to a toolkit I'd already been building for years. I bring both the technical depth and the business context to turn insights into actions.

I closed the business in 2023 to make this transition deliberately — completing a 12-month analytics certification and building out the case studies on this page rather than treating data as a side skill.
