For years I owned and operated a logistics business in Hamburg — optimising routes, forecasting demand, and segmenting customers using whatever data tools I had. Every decision had a real cost.

That pressure taught me something most analysts learn in theory: data only matters when it changes a decision.

What I learned running the business
Manual route planning cost real money — the kind of problem I now solve with optimisation models
Demand was seasonal and I under- or over-staffed constantly — see my forecasting case study
I could tell which customers were valuable but couldn't systematically act on it — this is exactly what the segmentation project addresses
