Forecasting, Real Estate01

US Housing Affordability

Where is housing becoming unaffordable, and how will that shift over the next 36 months?

2.22LA affordability index
36 moForecast with 95% CI
Business problem
Housing affordability is shifting unevenly across US metros, and investors and policymakers need to know where pressure will build next.
Dataset & scope
Metro-level income, housing cost and demographic data across major US cities, 2000 to present.
Approach
K-Means segmentation and SARIMA forecasting
Key finding
Los Angeles reaches a 2.22 affordability index, requiring $200K+ income.
Business implication
Affordability pressure is concentrating in coastal metros, widening the gap between income growth and housing costs.
Recommendation
Identify markets where income growth is failing to keep pace with housing costs.

Statistics, Labour Economics02

EU Gender Pay Gap

Is the EU gender pay gap closing, and where does it stay large?

10.5%EU average, 24.1% in Finance
3.9 to 11.2%Gap, under 25 to 35–44
Business problem
EU policy and HR teams need evidence of where the pay gap persists, and which groups are most affected.
Dataset & scope
19 years of Eurostat data (2006–2024) across sectors, age groups and member states.
Approach
Regression and t-tests on 19 years of Eurostat data (2006 to 2024)
Key finding
The gap closes at 0.33 points a year. Finance sits at 24.1% against 9.8% for every other sector.
Business implication
The gap narrows overall but widens sharply with age, and Finance remains a clear outlier.
Recommendation
Audit finance pay first and track the spread between countries, not only the average.

Supply Chain, Operations03

NYC Citi Bike Optimisation

How should bikes be redistributed across 1,500+ stations as demand shifts with weather and season?

20 to 25%Est. revenue opportunity
4 weeksAdvance forecasting
Business problem
Bike availability was mismatched to demand, leaving revenue on the table and frustrating riders.
Dataset & scope
Station-level ride data, weather and seasonality across 1,500+ NYC stations.
Approach
Forecasting model with a 0.82 temperature to usage correlation
Key finding
20 to 25% estimated revenue opportunity under a proposed dynamic pricing scenario.
Business implication
Demand is predictable four weeks ahead, which makes proactive redistribution a revenue decision, not a logistics one.
Recommendation
Use 4 week advance forecasts for proactive redistribution planning.

My story

From logistics founder to business analyst

For years I owned and operated a logistics business in Hamburg, optimising routes, forecasting demand, and segmenting customers using whatever data tools I had. Every decision had a real cost.

That pressure taught me something most analysts learn in theory: data only matters when it changes a decision.
